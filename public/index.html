Barcode & Magstripe Decoder

Paste raw AAMVA data from a PDF417 barcode or driver's license magstripe to decode it into readable fields. Powered by the py-aamva library.

Use of machine-readable identity data is subject to 18 U.S.C. chapter 123. Only decode cards you are authorized to read, and do not build databases from them without the holder's written permission.

Input

POST endpoint: /api/decode with {"data": "...", "format": "auto"}
